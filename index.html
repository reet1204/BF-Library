Library Rules

Library Rules

01
Annual subscription fee
Members are required to pay ₹500 as a yearly subscription fee to maintain active membership.
02
Subscription deadline
The subscription must be paid by the end of January of the ongoing year. Failure to do so may result in loss of membership.
03
Book issuance period
The maximum issuance period for any book is 2 weeks from the date of issue. Books must be returned within this time.
04
Freedom to issue
Members are free to issue any book available in the library catalog, subject to availability.
05
Book collection after approval
Once a book request is approved by the admin, the member must collect the book by the next Tuesday between 7:00 PM and 8:00 PM. Failure to collect within this window will result in the approval being cancelled and the book becoming available for others.

These rules are set by the library administration and are subject to change.
